Who can join Regather?

Families referred by an ER peer worker, clinic or nonprofit that works with Regather. They give you an invite code. There is no open sign-up.

Is Regather therapy?

No. People share what they have lived, not medical advice. Mentors are parents and young adults trained by your program.

Is my privacy kept from my parent?

No. Parents can never read a teen's pond or private chats, and teens can't read theirs.

What happens in a crisis?

If you write something that sounds unsafe, you see the crisis lines straight away: 9-8-8, Distress Centre Calgary and 9-1-1. A moderator at your program is told. Your message is never deleted.

What happens after 8 weeks?

You choose: keep the pond, join a new one, or move on and keep your private chats. Some families train to become mentors.

What if I need help right now?

Call or text 9-8-8, any hour. If someone is in danger, call 9-1-1.
